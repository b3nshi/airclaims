# Privacy notice

## Who is responsible

{{CONTROLLER_NAME}} ({{CONTROLLER_NIF}}), {{CONTROLLER_ADDRESS}}. Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## What we collect and why

- Account data (email, name, language): to give you access and contact you. Legal basis: contract.
- Claim data (flight, what happened, passengers, expenses, documents such as boarding passes and receipts): to prepare, send and track your claim. Legal basis: contract.
- Emails exchanged with the airline, AESA or others through your claim alias: to track your claim and forward replies to you. Legal basis: contract.
- Signature evidence (time, IP address, browser, a fingerprint of the text you signed): to prove what you agreed to. Legal basis: legitimate interest and legal obligations.
- Invoicing data if you receive compensation. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We only ask for an identity document when a specific step requires it. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising without your consent.

## Automated processing

We use AI to draft messages, classify incoming emails and read documents. Every message is approved by you before it is sent, and no decision with legal effects is taken automatically: the eligibility estimate is guidance, and uncertain cases are reviewed by a person.

## Who processes your data for us

Supabase (database and file storage, EU region), Vercel (web hosting), Forward Email (email delivery), our own automation server in the EU, and Anthropic (AI processing). Where a provider processes data outside the EEA, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

## How long we keep it

For as long as your claim is active and then for the limitation periods that apply to it (generally up to 5 years), plus invoicing data for the period required by tax law. Documents are deleted when that period ends.

## Your rights

You can access, rectify or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing and ask for portability by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
